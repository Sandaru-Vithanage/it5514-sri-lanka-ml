Model choices were fixed using three expanding chronological validation windows. SVR has the lowest test MAE; random forest has the lowest test RMSE. GPR is the assignment’s new method. Its small MAE difference from random forest is inconclusive under the paired block bootstrap.

Global explanation

Grouped feature reliance

Mean increase in test MAE after 20 permutations of seven-day blocks with a randomized phase. Correlated demand histories are shuffled together. Shuffling can still create unsupported combinations.

Evidence over a winning claim

GPR improves on the weekly baseline, but does not win every comparison. Its intervals cover fewer than the nominal 95% of outcomes, and unusual days can still produce large misses.

Sri Lankan source

PUCSL dispatch, through a versioned release

The raw Sri Lankan subset of Coombes, Hunt and Bloomfield’s SASEA dataset contains 2,902,337 plant-level records. It is shared under CC BY 4.0.

Reconstruct the energy target
Convert UTC to Asia/Colombo, remove 29,875 identical duplicate keys, integrate MW × 0.25 hours, and sum within local calendar days.
Apply quality gates
Exclude two partial boundary days and 12 days with explicit missing plant values. Require complete lag histories. Result: 756 modelling rows, split into 659 development and 97 test days.
Preserve the meaning
This is recorded dispatched energy, a demand proxy. Plant coverage changes; absent plants, rooftop solar, losses, supply constraints and publication delays limit operational interpretation.

New algorithm

A distribution over functions

GPR uses a covariance kernel to connect similar historical feature vectors. The fitted posterior supplies a mean forecast and a model-based observation interval.

Selected kernel

Constant × Matérn (ν = 2.5) + white noise

Predictors available at forecast time

Demand lags 1, 2, 7 and 14 days; previous seven-day mean and variability; cyclic weekday and annual position; time trend. Scaling uses development data only.

Use and limitations

An educational forecasting and explanation tool. It is not connected to grid operations. Evaluation is retrospective, covers one country and a short test period, and assumes complete previous-day telemetry.

The released daily CSV has a median published/reconstructed ratio of 4.041. This prototype uses independently reconstructed raw-data energy instead.
