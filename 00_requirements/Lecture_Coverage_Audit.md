## Novel-method decision

No occurrence of Gaussian Process/GaussianProcess was found in the extracted and OCR text across the supplied materials. Gaussian basis functions in a linear-regression notebook and Gaussian mixture clustering are different methods. GPR is therefore the selected new algorithm under the supplied-material evidence. It is a probabilistic kernel model with a posterior predictive distribution; an RBF kernel alone does not make it the taught SVM algorithm.

LightGBM and CatBoost are explicitly covered in XGBoost Algorithm.pdf; CatBoost also appears in Week 5. GAMs and explainable boosting machines appear in the Week 7 Explainable AI note. These are excluded as novelty candidates. All user-recalled algorithms are treated as taught or mentioned.

No assignment-specific prohibition or permission for AI assistance was found. Lecture practical exercise instructions (including RF/Gradient Boosting assignments) do not override the official brief.
